❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bible Adventure?
Bible Adventure is an interactive mini app for children. It helps kids discover Bible stories, characters, lessons, and Christian values through colorful visuals, simple language, and fun quizzes. It's not a PDF and not a traditional online course.
Who is it for?
It's made for young children and for the parents, grandparents, and family members who want to introduce them to the Bible in a way that keeps their attention.
What will my child learn?
Your child will explore well-known Bible stories like Noah's Ark, David & Goliath, and the story of Jesus, meet important Bible characters, and discover values such as kindness, courage, faith, forgiveness, obedience, and love.
Can my child use it independently?
The experience is designed to be simple and child-friendly, so many children can explore at their own pace. Younger children, or those still learning to read, may enjoy it most with a parent or grandparent alongside — which can also be a lovely moment to share together.
Is it a physical product?
No. Bible Adventure is a digital mini app. Nothing is shipped — you access it online.
How do I access it?
After your payment is complete, you'll receive your access details. You can open Bible Adventure from a phone, tablet, or computer.
Is the payment one-time?
Yes. It's a single payment of $7. There's no subscription.
Is it a replacement for the Bible?
No. Bible Adventure is a supplementary learning experience designed to help children become more familiar with Bible stories and Christian values. It works best alongside reading the Bible together as a family.
